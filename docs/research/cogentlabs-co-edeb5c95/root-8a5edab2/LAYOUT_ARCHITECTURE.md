# Layout Architecture

The site uses normal document flow beneath a fixed 80px header. All page sections own their background but place content inside `.shell` (max 1280px). Desktop grids use 12 columns; major text spans 7–9 columns. There are no floating cards or heavy surface layers. At 900px, multi-column content becomes two columns. At 700px, content becomes one column, gutters become 20px, the desktop nav hides, and the menu trigger appears.
